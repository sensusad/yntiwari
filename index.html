But, Who am i?

I am,

Hi,

I am Yuwaditya N. Tiwari.

My roots lie in the discipline of a Kumaoni Brahmin household, having my vision stretches toward horizons shaped by science, technology, business, and imagination. I do not see myself confined to one identity. I am a student in pursuit of wisdom, someone who tries turning ideas into action, a custodian who carries forward culture and values, and a thinker who questions.

For me, leadership is not the pursuit of recognition and power but the pursuit of responsibility and positive impact. I believe every action, whether reviving forgotten traditions, building technology that supports the community, or amplifying voices within my society, is a contribution to a greater context of service. The work I do is not different from who I am, it is the expression of an inner calling connected to the divine to align knowledge with purpose.

I carry pride in my heritage, for my country the great Nepal and this world have shaped my previous generation(s) into who they were, and through them, I stand where I am today. That legacy is my strength, my power, and their Aashirvad (blessings) for me. It reminds me that I do not walk this path alone; I walk it with the energy of generations before me. It is their spirit that gives me the will to contribute more, to my nation and to humanity at large.

Now, who am I?

I am a human in learning, a seeker of truth, and a contributor to my country and this world, ever striving to build bridges between past and future, tradition and innovation, self and the divine.
